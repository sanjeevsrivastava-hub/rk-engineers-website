About R.K. Engineers Sales Limited

R.K. Engineers Sales Limited has been a legend in the industry of water and waste water management solutions in all over India for over years and we continue to achieve excellence. We represent the most advanced and innovative manufacturers in the industry. Our hallmark is quality product & quality after sales services for individual problem solving with ultimate efficiency. We are representative and service provider for world’s leading manufacturers of equipment used in the water and wastewater segment. We have excellence in providing the complete solution in this segment. We serve the municipal, industrial, construction, commercial and utility markets. We compete by providing excellent service, outstanding products and sound technical support and are committed to the highest standards of ethical business conduct. R.K. Engineers Sales Limited was established by Mr. Ravi Jain (Managing Director) in the year 1978 with a small business of supply of pump spares and tube well accessories in U.P. Irrigation Department and later specialised in selling of KSB make submersible pumps. We became Private Limited in the year 2001 and Limited Company in the year 2011, having a strength of office, sales & service team of more than 200 professional staff. At present our company is certified as an ISO 9001 : 2015 company and has order booking of more than 300 crores in a calendar year. During our journey, we achieved many milestones and were awarded for excellence in our work. We take pride in being a company which provides such working environment that fosters teamwork and long-term relationships, educated, healthy and dedicated employees that will serve our customers with distinction. We always intend to upgrade ourselves and our employees by adopting the latest technology and products of the present scenario for providing the best user friendly and cost-effective solutions to our clients as per their requirement.

As an Authorized KSB Pump Sales & Service Partner, R.K. Engineers provides genuine pump products, technical support, repair solutions and after-sales services to customers.

With experienced engineers, skilled technicians and a dedicated pump repair workshop, the company delivers reliable solutions for water supply, wastewater management, sewage pumping and pressure boosting applications.

Our focus is to provide quality products, professional service support and long-term customer satisfaction through technical expertise and commitment.
